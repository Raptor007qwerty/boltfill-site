Support

Found a bug, or something not behaving the way you expected? Please tell us — reports are genuinely useful while Boltfill is still pre-launch.

Report a bug or ask a question

The best place is the issue tracker for this website's repository on GitHub. It is public, so anyone can open an issue and follow the reply.

Open an issue on GitHub

Prefer email? Write to plugins.dns@gmail.com.

What to include

A report is much easier to act on when it says:

  1. What you did, step by step.
  2. What you expected to happen.
  3. What happened instead.
  4. The website or kind of text box it happened in, if it was site-specific.
  5. Your Chrome version, and the Boltfill version from the settings page.

Please do not paste snippet contents, passwords, card numbers or other personal data into an issue. Issues are public. A short made-up example that reproduces the same problem is just as useful.

Reporting a security problem

Please do not open a public issue for a security vulnerability. Email plugins.dns@gmail.com with "Boltfill security" in the subject, and include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it.

Common questions

Where can I install Boltfill?

Not anywhere yet. Boltfill is a pre-launch release candidate and has not been submitted to the Chrome Web Store. This page will be updated when that changes.

Nothing expands when I type my shortcut.

Check three things: that Boltfill has been granted access to the site (open the popup and click Enable), that it is not paused in Settings, and that the site is not on your exclusion list. Boltfill also deliberately never expands in password, card number, CVV or one-time-code fields.

My shortcut only expands after I press space.

That happens when one shortcut is the beginning of another — with both /th and /thanks saved, /th has to wait so that /thanks stays typeable. A setting also switches everything to wait-for-space if you prefer it that way.

Are my snippets synced between computers?

No. They are stored locally on each device and are not synced to your Google account. Use Settings → Backup to export them to a JSON file and import it elsewhere.

How do I get my data out, or delete it?

Settings → Backup → Export writes a plain JSON file to your computer. Settings → "Delete everything" removes all snippets and settings from the device, and uninstalling the extension removes its storage with it.

Which websites does it work on?

Boltfill works in ordinary form fields, textareas and rich text editors on sites you have granted access to. Compatibility with specific named sites is still being tested and is deliberately not claimed until it has been verified.

Can I stop it running on one particular site?

Yes. Settings → "Never run on these sites" excludes a site and its subdomains entirely. Site owners can also mark a region of a page with data-boltfill="off".

Privacy

Boltfill stores your snippets and settings locally on your device, sends nothing over the network, and collects no analytics. The full detail is in the privacy policy.
